07 / Our trust and transparency

Know who you are using.

We want you to understand what IDBridge is, where our information comes from and what we do not do.

👥

Who we are

IDBridge is an independent citizen guidance project. We are not the official NIRA website.

🔗

Our sources

Guides show their official source, link, review date, reviewer and version.

🛡️

Our privacy promise

Guidance does not require your personal number, ID photo, fingerprints, one-time code or password.

🧪

Testing and reliability

Our current automated suite has 7 passed and 0 failed tests. Security review and user pilot results are not yet complete.

See something wrong? Please check the official NIRA source before acting. You can also tell us about an incorrect guide. Do not include your personal number or ID documents.

A helpful distinction

We make the path clearer.
Official institutions resolve the case.

IDBridge is independent. We do not issue IDs, see NIRA records or ask for private information.
