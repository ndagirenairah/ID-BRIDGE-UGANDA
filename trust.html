👥

Who we are

IDBridge UG is an independent citizen guidance project. We explain public information in simple language and help people find their next step.

We do not issue National IDs, make decisions about applications or speak on behalf of NIRA.

🔗

Where information comes from

Our guides point to official sources. Each published guide shows:

  • the source and link
  • the date it was last checked
  • the reviewer
  • the guide version

Our promise

Guidance without exposure.

You can use IDBridge without creating an account or sharing identity details.

We do not ask forYour personal number, ID photo, fingerprints, one-time code or password.
We do not accessPrivate NIRA records, application status or identity databases.
We do not promiseThat a guide will change an application decision or resolve a delay.
We do providePlain-language guidance, checklists and links to official NIRA services.
